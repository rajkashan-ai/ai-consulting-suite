Pick 1a if

you want the suite to feel like a tool people trust with money. Cheapest to build on what exists, and the rail scales to the tools that are still coming.

Pick 1b if

the buyer is the owner, not the ops person. It is the warmest and the least like every other AI product, and the one that will look good in marketing shots.

Pick 1c if

the tracker is the hero and users will live in it daily. Highest ceiling, most work: it needs the evidence panel, heat scale and keyboard layer to be worth it.
